Meet Jeremy Esperant

Jeremy Esperant, Sales Associate | United Realty Group Inc.

Strategy first, every move

Jeremy Esperant is a South Florida local and a Florida-licensed Sales Associate with United Realty Group Inc., working out of the brokerage's office at 7300 W. McNab Road, Suite 119, Tamarac, FL 33321. He serves buyers and sellers across Broward County — Plantation, Sunrise, Fort Lauderdale, Tamarac, Lauderhill, Davie, Weston and Pembroke Pines — and neighboring Palm Beach County.

Before real estate, Jeremy spent years leading teams in customer-facing retail management, where he learned to listen first, explain clearly, and follow through. He brings that same approach to every transaction, along with a chess player's habit of thinking several moves ahead: anticipating inspection issues, appraisal gaps, condo-association questions and negotiation turns before they become problems.

Jeremy also works closely with homeowners trying to sell by owner (FSBO), offering honest pricing guidance and a clear picture of what full representation adds.

What clients can expect

  • A written game plan for your sale or purchase
  • Fast, direct communication — call or text 954-643-4239
  • Local pricing based on real Broward comps
  • Condo, 55+ and first-time buyer know-how

How I Play

I treat every deal like a match: plan three moves ahead, read the board (today's Broward market), and protect my clients' king — their money — with clear pricing, strong offers, and no surprise sacrifices.
